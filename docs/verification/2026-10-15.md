## Proven diagnostic defect and correction

The click recorder queried the first textarea. With notes first, it recorded notes as the explanation; when the explanation field was absent, it could silently substitute notes. Input events also lacked field identity. A trace contained event history but no current field state, so missing, hidden, and empty fields could not be distinguished after a timeout.

- Input/composition/change records now identify only `answer-text`, `notes`, or `<other>`; arbitrary element IDs are not exported.
- Click records read `answer-text` explicitly and use `<missing>` if absent, never another textarea.
- Retrieving evidence adds a current `snapshot`: explanation/notes presence, rendered visibility, allowlisted value, and current redacted storage. Visibility means a nonzero layout rectangle without hidden/collapsed visibility, not a guarantee of focusability, unobstructed interaction, or being inside the viewport.
- The snapshot is sampled only when evidence is requested. No layout reads, new waits, or retries were added to the rapid input/click paths. The existing 80-entry event cap, synthetic-text whitelist, independent failure teardown budget, and artifact upload allowlist remain unchanged. A full history does not prevent the current snapshot from being returned.
